The room

Brick, wood paneling and candlelight.

The house on South Third

Four things under one roof.

  1. I · The buildingA blacksmith shop and carriage house.Two stories of 19th-century brick a few steps off Warren. The wood-burning oven and fireplaces stayed.
  2. II · The floretA flower stall in the dining room.Fresh and dried flowers, seasonally sourced from the farm and Hudson Valley growers.
  3. III · The farmTwelve miles to the table.Honey and produce from their own farm outside Hudson; bread from Sparrowbush, cheese from Talbott & Arding.
  4. IV · The hearth“Like having a party here every night.”Jason Denton, to The Daily Gazette.

The Floret of Feast

Flowers, by the stem or the armful.

Fresh and dried, seasonally sourced from the farm and other Hudson Valley growers. Order a bouquet ahead for pickup, or stop in and they’ll make one. Design classes and small weddings too.
